🧘‍♀️ Hobby

Outside of studying the evolution/reproduction of microbes and human beings, I am a committed yogi. I started a few years ago with short routines to start my mornings, and yoga has gradually become an integral part of my graduate school life. I get to relax and recharge during slow flows, and challenge myself in a heat-generating vinyasa flow.

I also like taking walks in nature, swimming, and reading. Occasionally, I write for the Tri-I journal Natural Selection.
