🧘‍♀️ Hobby

Outside of research, I'm a fairly devoted (if occasionally wobbly) yoga practitioner. I started a few years ago looking for a break from staring at terminals and genome browsers, and it's since turned into one of my favorite parts of the week — part exercise, part moving meditation, part excuse to roll out a mat somewhere quiet and stop thinking about p-values for an hour.

I'm especially fond of slower, breath-focused practices, though I'll happily get humbled by a fast-paced vinyasa class too. Crow pose and I have a complicated relationship.
