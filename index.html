About

I'm a PhD student in the Hajirasouliha Lab at Weill Cornell Medicine, advised by Prof. Iman Hajirasouliha. My research is at the intersection of computer science and life science — I develop computational methods to study and model reproductive health medicine as well as human gut microbiome metagenomics.

Before Weill Cornell Medicine, I studied computer science at Cornell University and was supervised by Prof. Jaehee Kim and Prof. Anusha Shankar. I'm broadly interested in algorithms, statistical modeling, and how computation can accelerate our understanding of women health and evolutionary biology.
